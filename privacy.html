Privacy Policy

Swap – community driven space to share, love and care. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Your name and email (or your Google account name and email if you use those to sign in); the items you post, including photos; messages you send to other members; and, for identity verification, the type of ID you submit, the last 4 characters of its number, and a photo of the ID.

Why we collect it

To run your account, show your items to other members, deliver messages between members, and confirm that members are real people before they can post or message.

Who can see it

Your name, items and verification badge are visible to other signed-in members. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Your ID photo and ID details are visible only to you and the Swap administrators who review them. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. ID photos are deleted after verification is complete or when you ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting and database provider, Supabase, and the website is hosted by Netlify. Sign-in with Google is handled by those companies under their own privacy policies.
